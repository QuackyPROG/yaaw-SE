# Research admission

## Purpose
Keep external research and host-provided expertise evidence-driven rather than letting available tools silently choose architecture.

## Admission basis
Vendor-, framework-, package-, or platform-specific research is allowed only when at least one basis is explicit in the current durable frontier:
1. observed repository technology;
2. accepted product constraint;
3. accepted `ENG-*` decision;
4. an explicit current-frontier candidate under evaluation.

Availability of a Codex/host skill is not an admission basis.

## Candidate-neutral rule
When architecture itself is unresolved, establish decision criteria before vendor-specific research. Compare only candidates that satisfy current product/repository constraints. Do not load a Next.js, Vercel, Supabase, database, framework, or provider skill merely because it exists and then treat that availability as architectural evidence.

## Source quality
Prefer primary sources: official documentation, specifications, upstream source, first-party APIs, authoritative release notes, and package-registry metadata. Record version/date for version-sensitive claims. Secondary sources may help discover primary evidence but do not replace it for material decisions.

## Durable research
A material external fact that blocks the current frontier belongs in `.yaaw-core/project/research/RSH-NNN.md`. One artifact answers one bounded question. Research may resolve facts, uncertainty, or conflicts; it does not itself create an `ENG-*` decision.

Planner promotes verified research into engineering truth only through normal decision recording with provenance.

## Host expertise
YAAW expertise in `registries/expertise.json` is canonical shared guidance. Provider/host skills are optional advisory expertise and may be loaded only after a research-admission basis exists. Host expertise never grants workflow authority or expands artifact writes.

## Stop rule
Do not research future fog. If a fact is not needed to make the next bounded frontier implementable, defer it.
