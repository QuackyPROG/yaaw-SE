# Inspect engineering quality

## Purpose
Inspect material engineering quality independently from contract correctness and test validity.

## Inputs
Current diff/repository standards, selected expertise, codebase-design guidance, and relevant security/accessibility/migration constraints.

## Checks
Check repository standards, unnecessary abstraction, boundary/locality defects, scope creep, and material maintainability/security/accessibility issues. Style preference or heuristic smell alone cannot fail review; a finding must identify concrete engineering impact.

## Output
Engineering-quality observations only.
