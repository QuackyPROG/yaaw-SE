# Classify findings

- `PASS`: contract satisfied with adequate evidence.
- `REPAIR`: implementation defect; ticket/spec remain valid.
- `REPLAN`: accepted ticket/spec/engineering decision is materially invalid or insufficient.
- `BLOCKED`: review cannot be completed because required evidence is unavailable.

Assign durable `F-NNN` IDs and severity to findings. Prefer concrete evidence, expected behavior, actual behavior, and required repair/replan action.
