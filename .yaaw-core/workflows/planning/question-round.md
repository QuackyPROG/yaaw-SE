# Engineering question round

## Purpose
Resolve material engineering decisions at the current frontier without forcing fake precision into future fog.

## Inputs
Current product/engineering revisions, repository observations, and current decision frontier.

## Procedure
1. Ask at most 10 engineering questions answerable now.
2. Use A/B/C plus recommendation/reason when useful; accept free-form alternatives.
3. Cover architecture, data, interfaces, failures, security, UX constraints, migration, observability, or testing only when material.
4. Do not ask the human to decide routine reversible implementation details the Planner owns.
5. Do not reopen settled `ENG-*` decisions without new evidence or explicit request.

## Output
Question round awaiting human answers.
